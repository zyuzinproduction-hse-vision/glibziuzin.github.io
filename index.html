Start Research

Across UK construction, site sign-in, pre-shift briefings, POWRAs and PPE checks happen on every site, every day. You know how your own sites perform. What you cannot see is whether your procedures are typical, or what patterns appear across companies that run them differently.

This study answers the question: is the way pre-shift procedures are run connected to the level and nature of incidents on site. Not whether they happen — whether they change anything. The answers come from the people who run them and carry the responsibility for the outcome.

We are building technology for pre-shift worker readiness checks. For it to be worth anything, we need to know how the procedure works in practice, not how it reads in a method statement. So the study is open, and everyone who answers gets the report.
